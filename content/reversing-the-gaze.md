[about]
title = "About Reversing.Works"
text = "**Reversing.Works** brings workers, unions, technologists and researchers together to investigate the digital systems shaping work. Workplace apps increasingly track workers, collect data, allocate tasks, measure performance and make decisions that affect pay and working conditions. Our aim is to make digital investigation accessible and reproducible, so workers' organisations, unions, technologists and researchers can build on an established methodology rather than starting from scratch."
